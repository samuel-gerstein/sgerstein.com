Eight-Month Reading Plan

35 weeks · 12 books · 21 papers and articles · about 80 hours · Nov 2, 2026 to Jul 4, 2027

Your library keeps coming back to one question: how do you hold big systems accountable to the people inside them? The books you added today and your Zotero papers trace that line from cybernetics, through critical systems thinking, to AI auditing. This plan follows it, after two months of mostly fiction to get the habit back.

How to use it

  • Read 30 minutes a day, five days a week. Keep it to 20 or 25 minutes for the first month.
  • Commit to phase 1 only. Decide about the rest at the end of week 8.
  • If a book isn't working after 50 pages, drop it and take something of similar length from the shelf.
  • Use the two catch-up weeks if you fall behind. If you don't need them, read anything you like.

Before you start

  • Three books are EPUB only, so convert them in Calibre before sending them to your Kindle. They're tagged below.
  • Thirteen papers have no PDF in Zotero yet. They're tagged below, and eleven have a DOI link.
  • Ulrich 1981 and Beer 1983 have no DOI, and no copy turned up online. Try interlibrary loan early. If they haven't arrived by week 16, read Espinosa on its own.
